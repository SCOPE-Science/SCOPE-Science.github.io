THE SURVEY APPROACH

The research engine behind SCOPE.

SCOPE Survey is the wide-field research architecture within SCOPE, built around an evolutionary approach to how AI agents reason, collaborate, and check their work.

SCOPE Survey is being evolved to drive SCOPE’s mathematical sky survey: reliable progress across many questions, with short research windows and economical inference use.

Like a wide-field observing programme, it seeks useful discoveries throughout a broad landscape—not only at its most famous landmarks.

01 / EVOLUTION

Test. Select. Evolve.

Candidate architectures are tested on a shared benchmark, with each result independently audited. Selection prioritizes correctness, then inference cost. Strong parents are retained, and successful and failed research trajectories inform the next generation of general-purpose improvements.

02 / COORDINATION

Local work. Global perspective.

A meta lead coordinates research teams and independent advisors, combining parallel work on details with a search for simplifying structures.

03 / REVIEW

A claim is a starting point.

SCOPE’s separate review process assesses the resulting argument. Selected valuable results are intended to receive formal verification.

SCOPE Survey I is currently undergoing architecture evolution and evaluation. SCOPE is preparing for first light; the records currently shown are demonstration examples. Evolution here changes the research workflow, not the underlying model’s weights.
