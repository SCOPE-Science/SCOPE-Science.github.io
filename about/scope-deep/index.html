DEEP-FIELD RESEARCH

Stay with the difficult question.

SCOPE Deep is the deep-field research architecture within SCOPE, built for sustained investigation of harder and higher-value mathematical problems.

SCOPE Deep is a planned architecture for deep-field research: sustained investigation of harder, higher-value mathematical problems. It will devote more tokens and longer research windows to a smaller number of carefully chosen questions.

The emphasis shifts from covering ground to sustained investigation: revisiting an obstruction, developing a missing idea, and carrying a difficult argument through to completion.

01 / TARGET

Fewer, deeper investigations.

Focus on problems whose importance justifies a larger research commitment, beyond the short-window survey setting.

02 / EVOLUTION

Evolving for depth.

SCOPE Deep is intended to be developed through repeated proposal, testing, independent review, and selection of candidate architectures. Longer research windows and larger inference budgets will give ideas room to mature; they do not guarantee a solution.

03 / EVIDENCE

The same standard of proof.

Longer exposure does not relax the requirements for correctness, originality, or an explicit account of what has and has not been established.

SCOPE Deep’s evolution programme is planned, not yet an operating service or an achieved performance result. Evolution here changes the research workflow, not the underlying model’s weights.
