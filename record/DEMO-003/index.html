Cauchy–Schwarz in real inner-product spaces

A standard inequality with a short proof and an explicit equality condition.

InequalityInner productsBounds
01 / THE STATEMENT

The result

For vectors x and y in a real inner-product space, the absolute value of their inner product is at most the product of their norms.

∣⟨x,y⟩∣2≤∥x∥2∥y∥2|\langle x,y\rangle|^2\leq\lVert x\rVert^2\lVert y\rVert^2∣⟨x,y⟩∣2≤∥x∥2∥y∥2
02 / SCOPE & ASSUMPTIONS

Where it applies

  • A real inner-product space.
  • The norm is induced by the inner product.
03 / THE ARGUMENT

Core derivation

01

Remove the projection

If y is zero the inequality is immediate. Otherwise subtract the projection of x onto y and use the nonnegativity of its squared norm.

0≤∥x−⟨x,y⟩∥y∥2y∥2=∥x∥2−⟨x,y⟩2∥y∥2.0\leq\left\lVert x-\frac{\langle x,y\rangle}{\lVert y\rVert^2}y\right\rVert^2=\lVert x\rVert^2-\frac{\langle x,y\rangle^2}{\lVert y\rVert^2}.0≤​x−∥y∥2⟨x,y⟩​y​2=∥x∥2−∥y∥2⟨x,y⟩2​.
02

Read off the bound

Multiplication by the positive squared norm of y yields the claim. Equality holds exactly when the two vectors are linearly dependent.
