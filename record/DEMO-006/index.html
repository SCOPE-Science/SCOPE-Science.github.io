Energy conservation for the harmonic oscillator

A conservation statement with its domain and governing equation attached to the result.

Conservation lawDynamicsDifferential equations
01 / THE STATEMENT

The result

For a twice-differentiable real solution of q̈ + ω²q = 0 with constant ω > 0, the quantity E = (q̇² + ω²q²)/2 is constant in time.

02 / SCOPE & ASSUMPTIONS

Where it applies

  • q is twice differentiable on an interval.
  • ω is a positive constant.
  • q satisfies the unforced harmonic oscillator equation.
