DEMO-002 / Number theory / Demonstration

The infinitude of prime numbers

An existence result whose proof produces an integer with a prime factor beyond any proposed finite list.

ExistencePrimesDivisibility
01 / THE STATEMENT

The result

For every finite list of primes, there exists a prime outside that list.

#{p:p is prime}=∞\#\{p:p\text{ is prime}\}=\infty#{p:p is prime}=∞
02 / SCOPE & ASSUMPTIONS

Where it applies

  • Primes are positive integers greater than one.
  • Every integer greater than one has a prime divisor.
03 / THE ARGUMENT

Core derivation

01

Build an obstruction to completeness

Given distinct primes p₁, …, pₖ, form one more than their product. This integer is greater than one and therefore has a prime divisor q.

N=∏i=1kpi+1.N=\prod_{i=1}^{k}p_i+1.N=i=1∏k​pi​+1.
02

The divisor is new

For every listed prime, N has remainder one. Consequently q cannot be on the original list. No finite list contains all primes.
