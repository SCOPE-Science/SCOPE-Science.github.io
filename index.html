A Mathematical Sky Survey

A systematic search through the open questions between mathematics’ landmarks.

SCOPE develops and deploys evolved AI research architectures to explore mathematics at two scales: broad survey work and sustained deep-field investigation.

How SCOPE works → Search the findings on Resultary ↗
01 / PURPOSE

A research map,
not a publication stream.

SCOPE surveys the mathematical landscape systematically rather than competing for attention one result at a time.

Most findings are intended to remain quiet public records, becoming visible primarily when they are relevant to a researcher exploring the corresponding region of mathematics.

02 / RESEARCH ARCHITECTURES

Two ways of looking.

Both architectures are shaped through repeated testing, independent audit, and selection—not fixed by hand at first design.

03 / EVOLUTION

Architecture,
evolved.

Research workflows improve through evidence. Candidate architectures face shared mathematical benchmarks; independently audited outcomes determine what survives.

  1. 01
    Test

    Run candidate architectures against the same problems and bounded resources.

  2. 02
    Audit

    Review claims independently, including failed and incomplete research trajectories.

  3. 03
    Evolve

    Carry forward the structures that improve correctness, coverage, and inference cost.
